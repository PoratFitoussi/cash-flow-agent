# Surface Brief: Dashboard (index.tsx)

## Scope and Visitor Mode
Mode: Operate. The user is here to check cash flow, manage uncategorized transactions, and see spending pacing.

## Audience, Job, Action
Audience: Porat and Liza. 
Job: Understand available budget, review and approve AI categorizations.

## Direction contract
**THESIS:** The dashboard is an instrument panel in the dark. It tells you exactly what you need to know to fly the plane, and nothing more.

**OWN-WORLD:** Matte black panel ground. Gauges and text are luminous white. Active states glow radium-green. Amber and red lamps only for caution.

**STORY:** The visitor understands their exact financial standing at a glance and executes filtering and categorization with zero friction, trusting the instruments.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The hero aggregate cards become dial instruments (Income vs Outflow limits). The transactions list sits below like a terse flight log.

**FORM:** The Night Flight "Six-Pack"

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
